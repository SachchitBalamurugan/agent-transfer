I just open sourced a tool
that lets you take your setup
from Claude Code to Codex,
or the other way around.
Say you’ve spent weeks teaching Claude how you work.
You’ve installed skills,
written project instructions,
and figured things out in chats you still refer back to.
Now you want to try Codex.
Just ask it to bring your setup over.
It shows you what can transfer
and what needs a tweak.
You can even find an old conversation
and pick up where you left off.
